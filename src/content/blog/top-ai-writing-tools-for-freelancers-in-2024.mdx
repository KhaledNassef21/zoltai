# Top AI Writing Tools for Freelancers in 2024

## Quick Summary

🚀 **Key Takeaways:**
- **AI writing tools** like Jasper AI, Copy.ai, and Writesonic can speed up drafting and editing for freelancers
- **Jasper AI** is built for long-form content and brand voice
- **Copy.ai** focuses on marketing copy and workflows
- **Writesonic** has a free plan and lower-priced paid tier, which suits beginners
- Many writers combine 2-3 tools rather than relying on one

---

In 2024, AI writing tools are changing how freelance writers draft, research and edit. Used well, they can save time on first drafts and repetitive formats, while the writer stays responsible for quality, accuracy and voice.

This guide compares **Jasper AI**, **Copy.ai**, and **Writesonic**: what each one does, what it costs, and which kind of work it fits. Results depend on your skills and effort, and no tool guarantees clients or income.

## What AI Writing Tools Help With

**Freelance writers** commonly use AI writing tools for:
- Outlining and first drafts of blog posts and product descriptions
- Research summaries and idea generation
- Email and social media copy variations
- Rewriting and tightening existing text

Human editing is still essential: check facts, adjust tone, and make sure the final piece is original.

## Jasper AI: Best for Long-Form Content and Brand Voice

### Why Jasper AI Stands Out

**Jasper AI** is aimed at writers and teams who produce long-form content with a consistent voice.

**Key Features:**
- **Boss Mode** for comprehensive long-form content
- Includes over **50 AI templates**
- Integrated **plagiarism checker**
- **Brand voice training**
- Built-in **SEO tools**

### Pricing for Jasper AI in 2024
- **Starter Plan:** $39/month for 20,000 words
- **Boss Mode:** $99/month for full features
- **Business Plan:** Custom pricing

### Where Jasper AI Fits

- Long-form pieces such as white papers and in-depth articles
- Custom templates for repeatable formats like LinkedIn posts and email campaigns
- Teams that need the same brand voice across many pieces

## Copy.ai: Strong Option for Marketing Copy

### How Copy.ai Works

**Copy.ai** is built around short-form, persuasive copy.

**Key Strengths:**
- Includes over **90 AI templates**
- **Workflow builder** for complex tasks
- Consistent **brand voice**
- A/B testing suggestions
- Efficient social media content creation

### Pricing for Copy.ai in 2024
- **Free Plan:** 2,000 words/month
- **Pro Plan:** $36/month for unlimited content
- **Team Plan:** $186/month for teams

### Where Copy.ai Fits

- Email sequences such as welcome series and product launches
- Social media posts, captions and ad variations
- Landing page and product description drafts

## Writesonic: A Low-Cost Place to Start

### Beginner-Friendly AI Writing Software

**Writesonic** is an affordable option for people new to AI writing tools.

**Key Features:**
- **ChatSonic** built on GPT-4
- **Article Writer 5.0**: Generates lengthy articles
- **Photosonic**: AI image creator
- **Audiosonic**: Text-to-speech tools
- **Chrome extension** for writing convenience

### Writesonic Pricing for 2024
- **Free Plan:** 10,000 words/month
- **Pro Plan:** $12.67/month
- **Enterprise:** Custom pricing

### Where Writesonic Fits

- Learning how AI drafting works on the free plan
- Blog drafts with Article Writer 5.0
- Bulk product descriptions and social captions

## Combining Tools

Some writers use more than one tool for different jobs:

**Example tool stack:**
1. **Writesonic** for drafting
2. **Copy.ai** for marketing copy
3. **Jasper AI** for complex content
4. **Notion AI** for notes
5. **Surfer SEO** for keyword research

**Approximate cost of the full stack:** about $150/month at the plan prices above. Most people do not need all five, so start with one.

## A Simple Way to Get Started

### Week 1: Setup
- Sign up for **Writesonic** (free plan)
- Write a few practice pieces and compare the AI draft with your edited version
- Learn the tool's templates and settings

### Week 2: Try a Second Tool
- Test **Copy.ai** on a short-form task such as an email or social post
- Note which tool saves you the most editing time

### Week 3: Refine Your Workflow
- Decide which paid plan, if any, is worth it for the work you do
- Save templates and prompts you reuse

### Week 4: Review
- Compare time spent and quality across tools
- Try **Jasper AI** if you write long-form content and want brand voice training

## Avoid Common AI Writing Tool Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Treating AI as a Complete Solution
**Avoid:** Publishing AI output as-is
**Approach:** Edit carefully and fact-check

### Mistake 2: Hiding AI Usage
**Avoid:** Being secretive about it
**Approach:** Be open about how you use AI, and follow any client or platform rules on AI-generated content

### Mistake 3: Using One Tool for Everything
**Avoid:** Forcing a tool onto work it was not built for
**Approach:** Match the tool to the task

### Mistake 4: Skipping Originality Checks
**Avoid:** Assuming AI text is unique
**Approach:** Run a plagiarism check, such as the one built into Jasper AI
