Meetings are where decisions get made, and nobody wants to be the person typing notes. AI notetakers can record a call, write the transcript and send a summary. Here are three options, described from each vendor's documented features and current plans.

## Quick Summary
- Otter.ai for live notes and AI chat across your meetings, with a free Basic plan.
- Fireflies.ai for notes that connect to your other apps, with a free plan.
- Fathom for a free plan with unlimited recordings and AI summaries.

## 1. Otter.ai
Otter joins Zoom, Microsoft Teams and Google Meet calls, writes the transcript live and produces a summary. You can also ask its AI Chat questions about a single meeting or across meetings.

**Example:** After a client call, ask Otter AI Chat for the decisions that were made and who owns each follow-up.

**Pricing:** The free Basic plan includes 300 transcription minutes per month, up to 30 minutes per conversation and 3 lifetime audio or video file imports. Pro is $16.99 per month, or $8.49 per month with annual billing, and raises the limits to 1,200 minutes per month and up to 90 minutes per meeting (as of November 22, 2026).

## 2. Fireflies.ai
Fireflies records meetings on Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams and other platforms, transcribes them and writes searchable notes. Paid plans add app and CRM integrations.

**Example:** Search across past meetings for the day a customer asked about delivery times.

**Pricing:** A Free plan is available with limited storage and AI summaries. Pro is $10 per user per month billed annually ($18 month to month), and Business is $19 per user per month billed annually ($29 month to month). Some AI features use AI credits, which can cost extra on Business (as of November 22, 2026).

## 3. Fathom
Fathom records calls on Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams and gives you an AI summary and action items as soon as the call ends. Its iOS app can also capture in-person meetings.

**Example:** Send the instant summary to a teammate who missed the call.

**Pricing:** The Free plan has unlimited recordings and AI summaries. The Team plan is $15 per user per month billed annually ($19 monthly) with a 2-user minimum, and Business is $25 per user per month billed annually ($34 monthly) (as of November 22, 2026).

## Steps to Get Started
1. Tell everyone on the call that a notetaker is recording, and get consent where your area requires it.
2. Start with the free plan of one tool and check its limits against your meeting length.
3. Run it on three real meetings and compare the summaries with your own notes.
4. Read every summary before you share it, since AI can miss or misstate details.
5. Upgrade only if the free limits get in your way.

## The Bottom Line
Start with a free plan and a few real meetings. Free plans have limits on minutes, storage or AI features, so check each vendor's current pricing page before you commit.
