Missed calls are missed revenue, and hiring a full-time receptionist is out of reach for most small businesses. An AI answering service picks up every call, answers questions about your business, books appointments and takes messages, around the clock. Here are three worth a look, chosen based on their documented features.

## Quick Summary
- Smith.ai for a trainable AI receptionist with a free plan, from $150 per month for 75 calls.
- Goodcall for unlimited call length billed per unique customer, from $79 per month.
- Rosie for simple minute-based plans, from $49 per month.

## 1. [Smith.ai](https://smith.ai)
Smith.ai's AI Receptionist answers, qualifies and routes calls 24/7. You can train it on your business, test it in its Quality Studio before going live, and every call comes with recording, transcription and summaries. It integrates natively with tools like Clio and Zapier, and a hybrid option adds human agents when a call needs one.

**Example:** A two-person plumbing company lets the AI receptionist answer every call, qualify the job type and text the owner a summary, instead of losing after-hours callers to voicemail.

**[Pricing](https://smith.ai/pricing/ai-receptionist):** There is a free-forever plan with 25 calls per month. Pro is 75 calls per month at $2.00 per call ($150 per month), and Enterprise is 300 calls per month at $1.67 per call ($500 per month) (as of November 24, 2026).

## 2. [Goodcall](https://www.goodcall.com)
Goodcall gives you an AI phone agent you shape yourself: you define the greeting, the questions it asks and what it does with each call, and its assistant Gabi can rewrite the agent from plain-English instructions. Billing is unusual in a good way: you pay per unique customer served, not per minute, so long calls cost the same as short ones, and spam and wrong numbers do not count.

**Example:** A hair salon sets up the agent to answer opening-hours and pricing questions and book appointments, and repeat callers only count once per month no matter how often they call.

**[Pricing](https://www.goodcall.com/pricing):** Starter is $79 per month for 100 unique customers (about 150 calls), Growth is $129 per month for 500 unique customers, and Scale is $299 per month for 2,000 (as of November 24, 2026). You can build and test a demo agent free and only pay when you connect a number.

## 3. [Rosie](https://www.heyrosie.com)
Rosie is a 24/7 AI answering service aimed at solo owners and small teams. It answers calls, takes custom messages, sends appointment links by text and detects spam automatically, with a website chat widget included. Plans are simple minute bundles, which makes monthly costs easy to predict.

**Example:** A solo electrician forwards missed calls to Rosie, which answers basic questions, texts the caller a booking link and sends a summary of every conversation.

**[Pricing](https://www.heyrosie.com/pricing):** Professional is $49 per month with 250 minutes, Scale is $149 per month with 1,000 minutes, and Growth is $299 per month with 2,000 minutes (as of November 24, 2026). Annual billing gives two months free, and there is a 7-day free trial.

## Steps to Get Started
1. Count how many calls you miss in a typical week and when they come in.
2. Start the free plan or trial of one service above and forward your missed calls to it.
3. Test it like a customer: call after hours, ask common questions and check the summaries.
4. Keep the plan only if it books real appointments or saves real callbacks in the first two weeks.

## The Bottom Line
Start with a free tier or trial and your real missed-call volume. Smith.ai fits businesses that want a trainable receptionist with human backup, Goodcall fits anyone with long or unpredictable calls thanks to per-customer billing, and Rosie fits owners who want simple minute-based pricing. Let it answer real calls for two weeks before you judge it.
