Tasks slip when they live in your head and in scattered messages. A project tool keeps everything in one place, and many now add AI features. Here are three options, described from each vendor's documented features and current plans.

## Quick Summary
- Trello for simple card boards, with a free plan for up to 10 collaborators.
- Asana for tasks and projects with AI helpers, with a free plan for 2 users.
- Notion for notes and project tracking in one workspace, with a free plan.

## 1. Trello
Trello organizes work as cards on boards. Its Standard plan lists AI features such as capturing to-dos from email and Slack, and its Premium plan adds AI to boards along with Calendar, Timeline and Table views.

**Example:** Forward a customer email to Trello and let it turn into a card on your "To do" list.

**Pricing:** Free covers up to 10 collaborators per Workspace, with unlimited cards and up to 10 boards. Standard is $5 per user per month billed annually ($6 monthly), Premium is $10 per user per month annually ($12.50 monthly) (as of November 22, 2026).

## 2. Asana
Asana tracks tasks and projects in list, board and calendar views. Its paid plans list AI Teammates and AI Studio, with usage limits on how many requests and credits are included.

**Example:** Set up a project for a client launch and let automations assign tasks when a status changes.

**Pricing:** The Personal plan is free for 2 users with unlimited tasks and projects. Starter is $10.99 per user per month billed annually, and includes 5 AI Teammates requests per user per month, up to 50 per account (as of November 22, 2026). Check the page for the monthly price.

## 3. Notion
Notion combines notes, databases and project tracking in one workspace. Its Business plan lists Notion Agent and AI Meeting Notes, and the Free and Plus plans include a trial of Notion AI.

**Example:** Build a simple project tracker database and use views to see tasks by owner and due date.

**Pricing:** Free is $0 per member per month, Plus is $10 and Business is $20 per member per month (as of November 22, 2026). Notion's Custom Agents are free to try, then $10 per 1,000 monthly Notion credits. Check which billing period the page shows.

## Steps to Get Started
1. Write down how you track work today and what keeps slipping.
2. Start with the free plan of one tool and add one real project.
3. Try the AI features on a small task and check the result yourself.
4. Invite one teammate and see if the board stays up to date for two weeks.
5. Upgrade only if the free limits get in your way.

## The Bottom Line
Start with a free plan and one real project. Free plans have limits on users, boards or AI features, so check each vendor's current pricing page before you commit.
