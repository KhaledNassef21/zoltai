Embracing AI music technology offers several benefits: you don't need a musical background or expensive gear, making them perfect for crafting beats, scores, and custom tracks.

## What You Can Make with AI Music Tools

**Common uses:**
- Background music for YouTube videos
- Intros and outros for podcasts
- Audio tracks for social media
- Soundtracks for ads and presentations

**Where AI music can be shared or licensed**
- Stock music sites such as [Pond5](https://www.pond5.com), [AudioJungle](https://audiojungle.net) and [Shutterstock Music](https://www.shutterstock.com/music) set their own rules for AI-generated tracks.
- Beat marketplaces such as [BeatStars](https://www.beatstars.com) and [Airbit](https://airbit.com) have their own licensing options and policies.

Read each platform's current terms on AI-generated content and licensing before you upload anything. Results vary, and no tool or platform guarantees any outcome.
