Email is still one of the simplest ways for a small business to stay in touch with customers. Many email tools now include AI writing help for subject lines and copy. Here are four options, described from each vendor's documented features and current plans.

## Quick Summary
- Mailchimp for a familiar all-in-one platform with a small free plan.
- Brevo for a free plan that includes an AI content generator.
- MailerLite for a simple editor and a free plan with automations.
- Constant Contact for built-in AI copy and phone and chat support.

## 1. Mailchimp
Mailchimp is one of the best-known email platforms. It combines email campaigns, signup forms, templates and automation flows in one place, with 300+ integrations.

**Example:** Set up a welcome email that goes out automatically when someone joins your list.

**Pricing:** A basic Free plan is for businesses with fewer than 250 contacts. The Standard plan includes generative AI features at no additional cost, and paid plans start with a 14-day free trial (as of November 22, 2026). Paid plan prices depend on your contact count, so check the current price for your list size.

## 2. Brevo
Brevo covers email and SMS marketing. Its AI content generator drafts subject lines and email copy and can adjust the tone, and the platform includes a drag-and-drop editor and templates.

**Example:** Ask the AI generator for three subject line options for your next sale announcement, then pick one.

**Pricing:** The Starter plan is free with no credit card needed, and Brevo notes a limit of 300 emails per day on the free plan. The Standard plan starts at $18 per month, billed monthly (as of November 22, 2026).

## 3. MailerLite
MailerLite is built to be simple. It offers campaigns, automations, signup forms and a landing page, plus three email editors (drag and drop, custom HTML, and simple).

**Example:** Build a signup form and a landing page, then send new subscribers a short welcome sequence.

**Pricing:** The Free plan allows up to 250 subscribers and 2,500 emails per month. The AI writing assistant is part of the paid Comfort plan, which starts at $12 per month; Power starts at $25 per month (as of November 22, 2026).

## 4. Constant Contact
Constant Contact includes an AI copy generator in every plan, along with templates, social posting and signup forms. The Standard plan adds an AI campaign builder, according to the vendor.

**Example:** Use the AI copy generator to draft a monthly newsletter, then edit it so it sounds like you.

**Pricing:** There is no free plan, only a free trial with no credit card required. Plans start at $12 per month (Lite), $35 per month (Standard) and $80 per month (Premium); the price depends on your contact count (as of November 22, 2026).

## Steps to Get Started
1. Decide what you need first: newsletters, automated welcome emails, or both.
2. Check the free plan or trial of one tool against your list size.
3. Use the AI writing feature for a first draft, then edit it in your own words.
4. Send one real campaign and look at the opens and clicks.
5. Review after a month: keep the tool if it saves you time, switch if it does not.

## The Bottom Line
Start with a free plan or trial and one real campaign. Free plans have limits on contacts or sends, and AI features are not always included, so check each vendor's current pricing page before you commit.
