Chasing paper receipts at the end of the month is a tax on everyone's time. AI expense tools scan receipts for you, match them to card transactions, flag policy violations and draft the reports, so the whole process shrinks to a quick review. Here are three worth a look, chosen based on their documented features.

## Quick Summary
- Zoho Expense for the cheapest full-featured plans, free for 3 users, from $3 per user per month.
- Expensify for simple pay-per-use pricing and mature AI receipt scanning, from $5 per member per month.
- Ramp for a free AI-assisted spend platform with corporate cards, from $0.

## 1. [Zoho Expense](https://www.zoho.com/expense/)
Zoho Expense covers the full expense workflow: AI-powered OCR receipt capture (including itemized capture in 38+ languages), mileage tracking via GPS, expense policies, approvals and direct accounting integrations. Its Zia AI layer adds agentic expense reporting and approvals, an ask-anything bot, an insights dashboard and AI-driven audits that can detect fraud and alcohol purchases.

**Example:** A five-person consulting firm photographs receipts on the go, lets Autoscan read them, and the owner approves a ready-made report instead of decoding a shoebox of paper.

**[Pricing](https://www.zoho.com/expense/pricing.html):** The Free plan is $0 for up to 3 users with AI receipt capture and 20 receipt scans per user per month. Standard is $4 per user per month billed monthly ($3 billed annually) with 200 scans, and Premium is $6 ($5 annually) with 1,000 scans (as of November 26, 2026). A 14-day free trial needs no credit card.

## 2. [Expensify](https://www.expensify.com)
Expensify is the veteran of receipt scanning: its SmartScan technology reads receipts automatically, matches them to card transactions and codes the expenses, while its Concierge assistant handles questions and nudges the process along. It connects to the major accounting systems, including QuickBooks, Xero, NetSuite and Sage Intacct.

**Example:** A small retail team forwards e-receipts and photographs paper ones; SmartScan codes everything overnight and the bookkeeper exports a clean report to QuickBooks.

**[Pricing](https://help.expensify.com/articles/new-expensify/billing-and-subscriptions/explore-plans-subscriptions-and-pricing/Understand-Expensify-Pricing):** The Collect plan is $5 per unique member per month on pay-per-use billing. The Control plan is $18 per member per month on an annual subscription ($36 per active member above your subscription size), or $36 per active member per month pay-per-use, and using the Expensify Card can bring Control as low as $9 per member per month (as of November 26, 2026). If you take the card discount, check what the price becomes without it.

## 3. [Ramp](https://ramp.com)
Ramp combines corporate cards with AI expense automation: automatic receipt collection and matching over SMS or Slack, AI-assisted expense reviews, policy checks that can auto-lock cards when compliance slips, and accounting automation with QuickBooks Online and Xero on the free plan. Travel booking, bill pay and budgets are built in.

**Example:** A ten-person e-commerce business issues Ramp cards with per-vendor limits; receipts get collected and matched automatically, and anything out of policy flags itself before the month-end close.

**[Pricing](https://ramp.com/pricing):** The Free plan is $0 per user per month with the core AI-assisted expense, card and accounting features. Plus is $15 per user per month plus a platform fee based on team size (20% off with annual billing) and adds AI coding for every field and multi-entity support; Enterprise is custom-priced with annual billing (as of November 26, 2026).

## Steps to Get Started
1. Count how many people submit expenses each month and how many receipts that means.
2. Start on a free plan or trial and run one real month of expenses through it.
3. Connect your accounting tool and check that categories land where your bookkeeper expects.
4. Add policies and card controls only after the basic capture flow works for everyone.

## The Bottom Line
Zoho Expense fits budget-conscious teams that still want deep AI features, Expensify fits businesses that want proven receipt scanning with simple pay-per-use billing, and Ramp fits teams ready to combine cards and expenses on a free AI-assisted platform. Start free with one of them and let a real month of receipts judge it.
