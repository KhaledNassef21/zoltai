Clear emails and accurate translations make a small business look professional. AI writing and translation tools can check your tone, rewrite sentences and translate documents. Here are three options, described from each vendor's documented features and current plans.

## Quick Summary
- Grammarly for tone and grammar checks, with a free plan.
- DeepL for translating text and documents, with a free version.
- Wordtune for rewriting sentences, with a free Basic plan.

## 1. Grammarly
Grammarly checks spelling, grammar and tone as you write in your browser and apps. Its plans list tone adjustment, full-sentence rewrites and AI prompts that generate text.

**Example:** Paste a rough customer email and ask Grammarly to make the tone more polite before you send it.

**Pricing:** Free includes 100 AI prompts a month. Pro is listed at $12 and includes 2,000 prompts per member per month, and a free Pro trial is offered. Check which billing period the $12 uses (as of November 22, 2026).

## 2. DeepL
DeepL translates text and documents. Plans differ in how many characters and files you can translate each month, and paid plans add glossaries for your own terms.

**Example:** Translate a supplier email, and add your product names to a glossary so they stay the same.

**Pricing:** The free version allows up to 50,000 characters per user per month. Paid plans are listed in euros with VAT included, starting at 7.49 euros per month with annual billing, with up to 300,000 characters on the Individual plan and 1,000,000 on Team (as of November 22, 2026, EU pricing page). Check the page for your region.

## 3. Wordtune
Wordtune rewrites your sentences so they sound clearer or shorter. It works in the browser and its plans differ in how many rewrites and summaries you get.

**Example:** Paste a long paragraph from your website and ask Wordtune for a shorter version.

**Pricing:** Basic is free with limited use. Advanced shows $4.89 per month billed annually, marked down from $6.99, and the free trial lasts 3 days (as of November 22, 2026). Check the price after the discount.

## Steps to Get Started
1. Pick one thing you write often, such as customer emails or product pages.
2. Start with the free plan of one tool and use it on three real messages.
3. Read every change before you accept it, especially names, numbers and translations.
4. Ask a native speaker to check anything important in a language you do not know.
5. Upgrade only if the free limits get in your way.

## The Bottom Line
Start with a free plan and three real messages. Free plans have limits on prompts, characters or rewrites, so check each vendor's current pricing page before you commit.
