### Step 3: Create a Sample

1. **Script:** Use ChatGPT to draft a 2-minute script, then edit it.

- ✅ Add background music when it helps.  
- ✅ Keep a transcript.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Robotic Audio:** Choose human-like voices and edit pacing.  
❌ **Skipping the Listen-Through:** Always check the final audio.  
❌ **Ignoring Pronunciation:** Fix names and terms before export.  
❌ **Ignoring Feedback:** Improve with listener comments.  
❌ **Poor Source Scripts:** Good audio starts with a well-written script.  
❌ **No Backup Plan:** Keep your scripts so you can switch tools if needed.
