Resumes pile up in your inbox and good candidates slip away while you are busy running the business. A hiring tool keeps every applicant in one pipeline, and the AI features help you sort and shortlist faster. Here are three options, described from each vendor's documented features and current plans.

## Quick Summary
- Manatal for affordable AI candidate matching, from $15 per user per month billed annually.
- Breezy HR for a visual pipeline with AI applicant scoring, from $157 per month billed annually.
- JazzHR for structured hiring with AI-powered candidate matching, from $1,000 per year.

## 1. Manatal
Manatal is an applicant tracking system built around AI. Its AI Advanced Search combines filters with semantic search to surface the best-matching candidates in your database, and candidate profiles are enriched with data from resumes and social media. You can automate emails and tasks as candidates move through your pipeline.

**Example:** Paste a new role into Manatal, publish it to your career page, and let the AI relevance scoring rank the applicants who already match your requirements.

**Pricing:** Professional is $15 per user per month billed annually (15 jobs, up to 10,000 candidates), Enterprise is $35 per user per month annually with unlimited jobs, and Enterprise Plus is $55 per user per month annually (as of November 23, 2026). A 14-day free trial needs no card.

## 2. Breezy HR
Breezy HR organizes hiring as a drag-and-drop pipeline. Its Breezy Intelligence features include AI scoring of applicants, and the trial comes with 100,000 Breezy Intelligence credits, enough to score about 200 applicants. SMS messaging and onboarding workflows are available as add-ons.

**Example:** Turn on AI scoring for a new opening and review the highest-scored applicants first instead of reading every resume in order.

**Pricing:** There is no free plan, but every feature can be tried free for 14 days with no credit card. Startup is $157 per month billed annually ($189 monthly), and Growth is $273 per month annually ($329 monthly) (as of November 23, 2026).

## 3. JazzHR
JazzHR focuses on structured, repeatable hiring: job posting and syndication, workflow helpers, resume parsing and AI-powered candidate matching. Plans are billed annually, and some features are consumption-based, so costs scale with your hiring activity.

**Example:** Post a role to multiple job boards at once, then use candidate matching and workflow helpers to move people from application to interview without spreadsheet tracking.

**Pricing:** Hero is $1,000 per year, Plus is $3,480 per year and Pro is $5,508 per year (as of November 23, 2026). Note that standard pricing does not apply to staffing agencies, franchises or organizations over 250 employees.

## Steps to Get Started
1. List the roles you hire for most and how many applicants each gets.
2. Start the free trial of one tool above and post one real job.
3. Run the AI scoring or matching on your first batch of applicants.
4. Keep the plan only if it saves you real hours in the first two weeks.

## The Bottom Line
Start with a free trial and one real job posting. Manatal is the budget-friendly AI option, Breezy HR is the visual pipeline with AI scoring, and JazzHR fits teams that want a structured annual plan. Pick the one your team will actually open every day.
