# Sell AI Art Online Using Midjourney & Leonardo: 2024 Guide

## Quick Summary

🎯 **Key Takeaways:**
- **Tools:** Midjourney ($10/month) for high-quality images; Leonardo AI (free tier) for beginners.
- **Where people list AI art:** Etsy, Creative Market, print-on-demand sites, and their own stores.
- **Check the rules first:** Commercial-use rights depend on the tool, your plan and each marketplace's policy on AI-generated work.
- **Startup cost:** Midjourney starts at $10/month; Leonardo AI has a free tier.
- **No guaranteed results:** Sales depend on your designs, marketing and the market.

This guide covers how to create AI art with Midjourney and Leonardo AI, and the practical details of listing it on common platforms.

## What You Can Make

**Common AI art formats:**
- Digital downloads such as wall art and quote backgrounds
- Prints through print-on-demand services
- Custom pieces such as pet portraits
- Social media templates

Building a consistent style and a small portfolio helps you learn what works before you invest more.

## Why Use Midjourney and Leonardo AI?

### Midjourney: Image Quality

- **High-Quality Art:** Produces detailed, polished images quickly.
- **Consistent Style:** Helps create cohesive collections.
- **Commercial Use:** Terms depend on your subscription plan, so read Midjourney's current terms before selling anything.
- **Pricing:** From $10/month.

### Leonardo AI: Beginner-Friendly

- **Free Daily Generations:** 30-50 images with 150 tokens.
- **Versatile Models:** Adapt to diverse art styles.
- **Customization:** Style adjustments at no extra cost.
- **Pricing:** Start free; paid tiers from $10/month.

## From Idea to First Listing

### Step 1: Choose a Theme

Specializing helps your collection feel consistent. Example themes:
- **Abstract Wall Art**
- **Artistic Quote Backgrounds**
- **Custom Pet Portraits**
- **Vintage Travel Posters**
- **Minimalist Home Decor**

### Step 2: Craft Effective AI Art Prompts

Structure prompts so results are easier to repeat:

`[Main Subject] + [Style] + [Tone/Color] + [Specs] + [Exclusions]`

**Example Prompts:**
```
Abstract waves, minimalist, warm gold and deep blue, clean layout, high contrast --ar 3:4 --no text, no humans

Vintage poster of mountain landscape, art deco, sunset hues, bold typography spot, professional design --ar 2:3 --no modern features

Motivational desk flat lay, modern style, bright light, clean design, text space --ar 16:9 --no clutter
```

### Step 3: Create a Collection

**With Midjourney:**
1. Subscribe ($10/month).
2. Generate art via Discord `/imagine` prompts.
3. Use `V1, V2, V3, V4` for variations.

**With Leonardo AI:**
1. Register free with 150 daily tokens.
2. Generate using "Leonardo Creative."
3. Set image sizes that fit your intended format.

### Step 4: Understand the Platforms

**Etsy:**
- Listing fee: $0.20/item. Transaction fee: 6.5% per sale.
- Check Etsy's current policy on AI-generated items and disclosure.

**Creative Market:**
- Takes a commission on sales (30-70% depending on terms).
- Review its content guidelines before applying.

**Own Store:**
- Platforms like Shopify ($29/month) give more control over branding, with your own costs.

Fees and policies change, so check each platform's current terms.

## Print-on-Demand and Digital Downloads

### Print-on-Demand

Print-on-demand services print and ship on order, so you do not hold stock.

**Popular POD Platforms:**
- **Redbubble:** Integrates easily, large marketplace audience.
- **Society6:** Focus on home decor and art prints.
- **Printful + Etsy:** More control over branding.

### Digital Downloads

Digital files have no production or shipping cost, but you still need clear listings and licensing terms.

**Examples:**
- AI art packs
- AI social media templates

## Avoid Common Mistakes

❌ Avoid flooding listings with near-identical designs.
✅ Keep iterating and adapt to feedback.

❌ Don't neglect SEO.
✅ Use **Semrush** for keyword research.

❌ Don't skip the rules.
✅ Read tool licenses, marketplace policies and copyright guidance for AI-generated images.

❌ Ignoring email can limit repeat visits.
✅ Offer a free download to encourage email subscriptions.
