Booking calls, moving meetings and finding time to focus take up a lot of a small business owner's week. AI scheduling tools can handle some of it. Here are three options, described from each vendor's documented features and current plans.

## Quick Summary
- Calendly for booking pages that let customers pick a time, with a free plan.
- Reclaim.ai for AI-planned focus time and habits on your calendar, with a free plan.
- Motion for an AI calendar and task planner, on paid plans.

## 1. Calendly
Calendly lets customers book time with you from a shareable booking page that checks your calendar for conflicts. Paid plans add unlimited event types, automations and reminders, and payment connections.

**Example:** Put your Calendly link in your email signature so clients can pick a call time without back and forth.

**Pricing:** The Free plan has one event type and one calendar connection. Standard is $10 per seat per month billed yearly and Teams is $16 per seat per month billed yearly. Calendly also lists an AI Notetaker and an AI assistant called Callie that can be added to Standard or Teams, in English only (as of November 22, 2026).

## 2. Reclaim.ai
Reclaim connects to your calendar and uses AI to schedule focus time, habits and buffer time around your meetings. Its Smart Meetings feature helps find times for meetings with several people.

**Example:** Ask Reclaim to protect two hours of focus time each morning and move it automatically when a meeting comes up.

**Pricing:** Reclaim lists a Free Lite plan with one calendar sync and one scheduling link. Starter is $10 per seat per month and Business is $15 per seat per month, and the pricing page shows a yearly option that saves 20% (as of November 22, 2026). Check the page for the current billing and attendee terms.

## 3. Motion
Motion combines a calendar, task list and AI planning. It lists AI Calendar and Meetings, AI Chat and an AI writer and editor among its features, and it plans your task list around your calendar.

**Example:** Add your weekly to-do list and let Motion place each task into open time on your calendar.

**Pricing:** Motion lists Pro AI at $19 and Business AI at $29 per seat per month, with an option to pay annually that saves 33%. AI features use credits, and Pro AI includes 7,500 credits (as of November 22, 2026). Check the pricing page to see which billing period the listed prices use.

## Steps to Get Started
1. List the time-wasters first: booking calls, rescheduling, or finding time to focus.
2. Start with the free plan of one tool and connect only one calendar.
3. Try it for a week and count how many back-and-forth messages it saves.
4. Check the booking page and reminders yourself before you share them with customers.
5. Upgrade only if the free limits get in your way.

## The Bottom Line
Start with a free plan and one real week of bookings. Free plans have limits on event types, calendars or AI features, so check each vendor's current pricing page before you commit.
