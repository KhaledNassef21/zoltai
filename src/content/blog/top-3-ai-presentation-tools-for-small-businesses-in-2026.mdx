Pitch decks, client proposals and team updates all need slides, and building them from scratch takes hours. AI presentation tools can draft the first version for you. Here are three options, described from each vendor's documented features and current plans.

## Quick Summary
- Gamma for turning a prompt into slides, with a free plan.
- Beautiful.ai for automatic slide design, with a 14-day free trial.
- Pitch for working on decks with a team, with a free plan.

## 1. Gamma
Gamma generates a presentation, document or simple website from a prompt. It can import PDF and PowerPoint files and export to PDF, PPTX, PNG and Google Slides.

**Example:** Type a one-line description of your quarterly update and edit the slides it drafts.

**Pricing:** The Free plan includes 400 credits at signup and up to 10 slides per prompt. Plus is $9 per month ($108 billed annually) with 1,500 monthly credits and removes Gamma branding. Pro is $18 per month ($216 billed annually) with 4,000 monthly credits (as of November 22, 2026).

## 2. Beautiful.ai
Beautiful.ai focuses on automatic slide design. Its Smart Slides auto-format your content, and it lists AI content generation, an AI writing assistant and AI translation among its features.

**Example:** Paste your talking points and let the Smart Slide layouts arrange them, then adjust the brand colors.

**Pricing:** Pro is $14.50 per month billed annually, and a 14-day free trial is offered. Teams is listed at $40 per month with annual billing ($50 billed monthly), and includes three users (as of November 22, 2026).

## 3. Pitch
Pitch is a presentation tool built for working on decks with others. It lists unlimited presentations, custom templates and sharing links, and it includes AI credits on its plans.

**Example:** Share a draft deck with a teammate and collect their edits in one place.

**Pricing:** Pitch lists a free plan with 500 AI credits, unlimited presentations and 2 external guests, and paid plans with 750 AI credits per month (as of November 22, 2026). Check the pricing page for current paid prices, which depend on seats and billing period.

## Steps to Get Started
1. Pick one real deck you need this week, such as a pitch or a client update.
2. Start with the free plan or trial of one tool.
3. Let the AI draft the slides, then rewrite the text in your own words.
4. Check every number, name and claim on the slides yourself.
5. Upgrade only if the free limits get in your way.

## The Bottom Line
Start with a free plan and one real deck. Free plans have limits on credits, slides or branding, so check each vendor's current pricing page before you commit.
