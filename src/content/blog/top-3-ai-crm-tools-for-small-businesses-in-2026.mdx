Leads get lost when they live in email threads and notes. A CRM keeps contacts and deals in one place, and many now add AI features for reports and emails. Here are three options, described from each vendor's documented features and current plans.

## Quick Summary
- Pipedrive for a simple visual sales pipeline, with a 14-day free trial.
- Zoho CRM for a free plan for up to 3 users.
- HubSpot CRM for free CRM tools you can start with.

## 1. Pipedrive
Pipedrive is a sales CRM built around a visual deal pipeline. Its Lite plan lists AI-powered report creation and meeting intelligence, and the Premium plan lists AI tools for writing, summarizing and replying to emails.

**Example:** Add each new lead as a deal and move it across the pipeline as you talk to them.

**Pricing:** Lite is US$14 per seat per month billed annually (US$168 a year), Growth is US$39 (US$468 a year) and Premium is US$59 (US$708 a year). Each plan has a free 14-day trial with no credit card required (as of November 22, 2026, US pricing page).

## 2. Zoho CRM
Zoho CRM covers leads, deals, email integration, calling and forecasting in one place. Its Professional edition lists AI agents that can run tasks for you, and the pricing page also lists built-in AI agents and free onboarding.

**Example:** Capture leads from a web form and have Zoho CRM add them to your sales pipeline automatically.

**Pricing:** Zoho CRM is free for up to 3 users. Standard is US$14 per user per month billed annually (US$20 billed monthly) and Professional is US$23 per user per month billed annually (US$35 monthly) (as of November 22, 2026, from Zoho's US edition comparison).

## 3. HubSpot CRM
HubSpot offers free CRM tools and paid Starter plans for marketing, sales and service. It lists AI agents and uses HubSpot Credits to pay for some of its AI features, with 500 credits included on Starter.

**Example:** Start with the free tools to track contacts and deals, and look at paid features only when you need them.

**Pricing:** The free tools cost $0 per month. Starter is listed at $7 per seat per month, and the pricing page also shows $20 per seat per month for another billing option. Check which billing period each price uses and what the AI credits cost beyond the 500 included (as of November 22, 2026).

## Steps to Get Started
1. List what you need to track first: leads, deals, follow-ups, or all three.
2. Start with the free plan or trial of one tool and import a small list of contacts.
3. Use the AI features for a first draft of reports or emails, then check them yourself.
4. Run your next ten leads through it and see if it saves you time.
5. Upgrade only if the free limits get in your way.

## The Bottom Line
Start with a free plan and ten real leads. Free plans and trials have limits on users, features or AI credits, so check each vendor's current pricing page before you commit.
