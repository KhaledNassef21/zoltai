Sorting receipts and matching bank transactions is a task most small business owners put off. Bookkeeping tools now use automation and AI features to do part of that work. Here are three options, described from each vendor's documented features and current plans.

## Quick Summary
- QuickBooks Online for automatic transaction matching, with a free plan.
- Xero for bank feeds and bill capture without per-user fees.
- Wave for simple invoicing and bookkeeping with automatic bank import on Pro.

## 1. QuickBooks Online
QuickBooks learns how you categorize income and expenses and then matches and records similar transactions for you. Its paid plans list Intuit Intelligence features, and several of them are marked as beta on the pricing page.

**Example:** Connect your business bank account and review the transactions QuickBooks has already matched and categorized.

**Pricing:** A Free plan covers one user, one bank connection and two invoices a month. Simple Start, Essentials and the next plan up list prices of $38, $85 and $140 per month, and the page shows discounted prices for new customers, such as $11.40 per month for Simple Start (as of November 22, 2026). Check what the price becomes after the discount ends.

## 2. Xero
Xero connects to your bank feed, reconciles transactions and captures bills and receipts with Smart Document Capture. Its plans have no per-user license fees.

**Example:** Photograph a supplier bill and let Xero capture the details, then match it to the payment.

**Pricing:** Early is usually $27 per month, Growing is usually $59 and Established is usually $97, in US dollars. The page shows heavily discounted prices for the first six months, so check the price you will pay after that (as of November 22, 2026).

## 3. Wave
Wave offers invoicing and bookkeeping for very small businesses. Its Pro plan lists automatic import of bank transactions, automatic merging and categorizing, and receipt capture.

**Example:** Turn on automatic bank import and check how Wave categorizes your first month of transactions.

**Pricing:** Pro is $190 per year billed annually. Wave also lists optional online payment fees of 2.9% plus $0.60 per credit card transaction on Starter, and a lower fee on Pro (as of November 22, 2026). The Starter plan price did not show on the page we checked, so see Wave's pricing page for it.

## Steps to Get Started
1. Write down what you need first: invoices, bank matching, receipts, or all three.
2. Start with a free plan or trial of one tool and connect one bank account.
3. Review how it categorizes your first month of transactions and correct mistakes.
4. Ask your accountant to check the setup before you rely on the reports.
5. Upgrade only if the free limits get in your way.

## The Bottom Line
Start with a free plan and one month of real transactions. Free plans have limits on users, invoices or features, so check each vendor's current pricing page before you commit.
