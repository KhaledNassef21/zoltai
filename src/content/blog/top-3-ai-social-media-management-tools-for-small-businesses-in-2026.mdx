Posting consistently is the part of social media most small businesses fail at, not the ideas. AI social media tools draft the posts, generate the images, pick the times and schedule everything, so one person can run every channel. Here are three worth a look, chosen based on their documented features.

## Quick Summary
- Buffer for the simplest per-channel pricing and a real free plan, from $5 per channel per month.
- Hootsuite for an all-in-one dashboard with AI writing and trend forecasting, from $99 per user per month.
- Predis.ai for AI-generated posts, videos and ads built from your brand kit, from $19 per month.

## 1. [Buffer](https://buffer.com)
Buffer keeps social media simple: connect your channels, queue posts and let its AI Assistant draft and rework captions. The free plan is genuinely usable, and paid plans are priced per channel, so a business with two or three channels pays very little. Analytics, a community inbox and first-comment scheduling are built in on paid tiers.

**Example:** A neighborhood bakery connects Instagram, Facebook and TikTok on the free plan, drafts a week of posts with the AI Assistant in one sitting and lets Buffer publish them on schedule.

**[Pricing](https://buffer.com/pricing):** The Free plan is $0 for up to 3 channels with 10 scheduled posts per channel and the AI Assistant included. Essentials is $5 per channel per month billed yearly and Team is $10 per channel per month billed yearly with unlimited team members (as of November 27, 2026). Paid plans come with a 14-day free trial.

## 2. [Hootsuite](https://www.hootsuite.com)
Hootsuite is the full dashboard: one calendar for all your accounts, one inbox for every message, and AI that generates posts and images, automates inbox replies and forecasts social trends up to 90 days ahead on higher tiers. It costs more than the others here, but it replaces several separate tools for a business that lives on social.

**Example:** A two-location gym runs ten social accounts from one Hootsuite calendar, uses AI-generated posts to fill gaps in the schedule and answers every DM from a single inbox.

**[Pricing](https://www.hootsuite.com/plans):** Standard is $99 per user per month billed annually ($149 billed monthly) for up to 10 social accounts, Professional is $199 per user per month with unlimited accounts and automation, and Advanced is $399 per user per month adding approvals and team workflows (as of November 27, 2026). There is a 14-day free trial.

## 3. [Predis.ai](https://predis.ai)
Predis.ai is built around generation: give it your brand kit (logo, colors, tone of voice) and it produces complete posts, carousels, videos and ads, then schedules them. A credit system meters the AI generation, and it can also analyze competitors' accounts to show what works in your niche.

**Example:** An online clothing shop sets up its brand kit once, then lets Predis.ai turn each new product photo into a ready-to-post carousel and short video, scheduled at the best times.

**[Pricing](https://predis.ai/pricing/):** Plans start at $19 per month billed yearly ($32 billed monthly) for one brand with 1,300 AI credits per month, $40 per month yearly ($79 monthly) for 4 brands with 3,200 credits, and $212 per month yearly ($249 monthly) for unlimited brands (as of November 27, 2026). Note the trial terms: the 3-day free trial requires a card and charges it when day 3 ends, so cancel before then if it is not for you.

## Steps to Get Started
1. List the channels you actually post on and drop the rest.
2. Start on Buffer's free plan or a trial and schedule one full week of posts.
3. Use the AI drafting for a first pass, then edit so the posts still sound like you.
4. After two weeks, keep only the tool that saved you real time.

## The Bottom Line
Buffer fits businesses that want simple, cheap scheduling with AI help, Hootsuite fits teams that want one dashboard for everything, and Predis.ai fits anyone who wants the AI to make the content, not just schedule it. All three have a free plan or trial, so test one with a real week of posting before you pay.
