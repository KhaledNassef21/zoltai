A proposal that takes three evenings to write is a deal that cools off. AI proposal software drafts the content, pulls in your pricing tables, collects the signature and tells you when the client opens it. Here are three worth a look, chosen based on their documented features.

## Quick Summary
- PandaDoc for a free entry plan and AI assistant connections, from $19 per seat per month.
- Proposify for sales teams that want approvals and AI writing, from $39 per user per month.
- Better Proposals for the cheapest per-user plans with tracking and payments, from $13 per user per month.

## 1. [PandaDoc](https://www.pandadoc.com)
PandaDoc covers the whole document flow: proposals, quotes, contracts, e-signatures and payments in one place, with a template and content library so you stop starting from scratch. Its newer AI angle is assistant connections: plans include access to AI assistants like ChatGPT and Claude through its MCP connector, so your AI tool of choice can work with your documents.

**Example:** A small IT consultancy builds a proposal template once, then turns each discovery call into a signed quote the same day instead of the same week.

**[Pricing](https://www.pandadoc.com/pricing/):** The Free plan is $0 for up to 5 documents a month. Starter is $19 per seat per month billed annually ($35 billed monthly) and Business is $49 per seat per month annually ($65 monthly); Enterprise is quoted per seat or per document (as of November 28, 2026). There is a 14-day free trial, and PandaDoc only charges you if you keep using it after the trial.

## 2. [Proposify](https://www.proposify.com)
Proposify is aimed at sales teams that want control over what goes out: user roles, approval workflows and account-wide metrics on higher tiers, with a content library and document insights showing how clients read your proposal. Every plan includes its AI features: an embedded AI writing widget to prompt, write, refine and condense content, plus an MCP server that connects Proposify to Claude for agentic workflows.

**Example:** A three-person marketing agency requires manager approval before any proposal goes out, uses the AI widget to tighten the wording, and watches the insights to see which pricing section clients reread.

**[Pricing](https://www.proposify.com/pricing):** Build is $39 per user per month billed annually ($49 billed monthly) with unlimited documents and signatures, Grow is $65 per user per month billed annually adding roles, approvals and API access, and Scale is $107 per user per month plus a $12,800 per year platform fee that includes 10 users (as of November 28, 2026). Each plan has a monthly send limit, and the 14-day trial needs no card.

## 3. [Better Proposals](https://www.betterproposals.io)
Better Proposals is the budget-friendly option: web-based proposals with e-signatures, payment collection through Stripe, GoCardless or PayPal, and open-tracking notifications on every plan. Its AI hook is an MCP server that lets your AI assistant look up documents and create proposals, and its Nudge feature keeps follow-ups on one screen so you always know who to chase next.

**Example:** A freelance web designer sends a proposal with a pay-now button, gets a phone notification when the client opens it, and takes the deposit before the follow-up call.

**[Pricing](https://www.betterproposals.io/pricing/):** Starter is $13 per user per month billed annually ($19 monthly) with 10 sends per month, Premium is $21 ($29 monthly) with 50 sends, and Enterprise is $42 ($49 monthly) with unlimited sends (as of November 28, 2026). The 14-day free trial needs no credit card.

## Steps to Get Started
1. Pull up your last three proposals and note what you rewrite every time - that becomes your template.
2. Start one free plan or trial and build that template in it.
3. Send your next real proposal through the tool and watch the open tracking.
4. Add signatures and payment buttons once the writing flow feels right.

## The Bottom Line
PandaDoc fits businesses that want one tool for proposals, contracts and signatures with a free start, Proposify fits sales teams that need approvals and AI writing, and Better Proposals fits solo owners who want tracking and payments at the lowest per-user price.
