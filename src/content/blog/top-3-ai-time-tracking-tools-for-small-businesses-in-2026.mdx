Nobody starts a small business because they love filling in timesheets. AI time-tracking tools record what you worked on automatically, sort it into projects and draft your timesheet for you, so billing hours stop slipping through the cracks. Here are three worth a look, chosen based on their documented features.

## Quick Summary
- Timely for fully automatic AI timesheets, from $11 per user per month.
- Toggl Track for a generous free plan and an anti-surveillance stance, from $9 per user per month.
- Clockify for free unlimited tracking with cheap paid upgrades, from $5.49 per seat per month.

## 1. [Timely](https://timelyapp.com)
Timely is built around automatic tracking: its Memory app records your work across thousands of apps and websites in the background, and AI categorizes that time and drafts your timesheet, which you review instead of building from scratch. It also covers project health monitoring, budgets, team planning and capacity reporting on higher tiers.

**Example:** A three-person design studio lets Timely record everyone's work automatically, then each person spends a few minutes on Friday approving an AI-drafted timesheet instead of reconstructing the week from memory.

**[Pricing](https://timelyapp.com/pricing):** Starter is $11 per user per month billed monthly ($9 billed yearly) for up to 5 users and 20 projects, Premium is $20 ($16 yearly) for up to 50 users, and Unlimited is $28 ($22 yearly) with unlimited users (as of November 25, 2026). A free trial is available.

## 2. [Toggl Track](https://toggl.com/track/)
Toggl Track mixes manual timers with automation: its desktop apps can track the apps and websites you use privately on your own machine, turn calendar events into time logs automatically, nudge you when a keyword linked to a project appears, and detect idle time. Toggl takes a documented anti-surveillance stance: no screenshots, no keystroke logging, no location tracking.

**Example:** A freelance consultant starts a timer from a calendar event, lets the browser extension auto-track client research, and sends a summary report with the invoice at the end of the month.

**[Pricing](https://toggl.com/track/pricing/):** The Free plan is $0 and free forever, for small teams (Toggl's FAQ says up to 3 users). Starter is $9 per user per month billed annually ($12 billed monthly) and Premium is $16 ($24 monthly) with timesheet approvals and profitability reports; Time Off Management is a $2 per user per month add-on and Capacity Management is $5 to $6 (as of November 25, 2026). Paid plans come with a 30-day free trial.

## 3. [Clockify](https://clockify.me)
Clockify's free plan is the draw: unlimited time tracking with a timer, timesheets, an auto tracker that privately records time spent on apps and websites, idle detection and a Pomodoro timer. Paid tiers add the business machinery: approvals, invoicing, attendance and overtime, QuickBooks integration, scheduling and expense tracking.

**Example:** A small cleaning company has the crew track jobs on their phones with the free plan, and the owner upgrades to Standard to approve timesheets and turn tracked hours into invoices.

**[Pricing](https://clockify.me/pricing):** The free plan includes unlimited time tracking, and Clockify's FAQ says you can invite up to 4 people for free. Standard is $5.49 per seat per month billed annually ($6.99 billed monthly), with Pro and Enterprise tiers above it (as of November 25, 2026). Annual billing takes 20% off, and a 7-day trial of the paid features needs no credit card.

## Steps to Get Started
1. Pick one tool and track only your own time for a week before rolling it out to a team.
2. Turn on the automatic tracking features and see how much of your week the AI categorizes correctly.
3. Compare tracked hours against what you actually billed last month to find the leaks.
4. Upgrade only when you hit a real limit, like approvals or invoicing, not before.

## The Bottom Line
Timely fits teams that want timesheets to write themselves, Toggl Track fits businesses that want automation without employee surveillance, and Clockify fits anyone who wants to start free and upgrade cheaply. All three have a free plan or trial, so test one on a real week of work before paying anything.
